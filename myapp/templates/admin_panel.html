How This Is Calculated

Deposit fees are the {{ site_cfg.deposit_fee_percent }}% held back from every wallet top-up (configurable in Settings). Net game house profit is computed from every bet placed and every payout credited across all 12 in-house games and wallet-paid spin packs — this money was never a separate transfer, it's simply the platform's share of gameplay, reported here for visibility. No real money moves anywhere by viewing this tab.
